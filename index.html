Experience, Tasveer

Tasveer × Pop Mart

Role
Events Coordinator
For
Tasveer Film Festival
Year
2025–26

The vision

South Asian culture is having a global moment, but collectibles barely reflect it, even as Gen Z and millennial diaspora fans drive collectible trends.

The idea

A limited-edition series of 3–5 Labubu figures inspired by South Asian film, in custom boxes drawn from film posters and cultural motifs, with QR codes linking to festival trailers.

Execution

Designed a 9-slide partnership deck: why now, why Tasveer, the concept, merch strategy, cross-promotion and next steps.

Experience, Tasveer

Tasveer Film Center signage

Role
Events Coordinator
For
Tasveer
Type
Environmental design

The vision

Give Tasveer's rebrand of the Ark Lodge a building that announces itself as a film center from across the street.

The idea

Two directions: a classic theater marquee in navy and gold that honors the building's history, and a cleaner modern take with a lowercase wordmark and vertical blade sign.

Execution

Mocked up both directions on a photo of the real facade, including the frieze lettering, marquee, reader boards and Tasveer's mascot on the wall.

Project, 2023–24

Viridescent Runway

Role
Producer & PM
Partner
Prairie Underground

151 guests. 60 volunteers. 40% profit recovered.

Back to contents

The vision

Show that sustainable fashion belongs on the red carpet.

The idea

"Walking the path of sustainability": one visual world carried from the gold-framed invitation and ticketing poster to the stage and a fashion magazine.

Execution

Designed the invitation, poster and Spring 2024 magazine featuring Prairie Underground, led a 60-person volunteer team and a social campaign on Instagram, WeChat and video, and secured a Seattle talent agency for the lineup.

Result

151 attendees, 40% profit recovered, and guests including the Mayor of Bellevue and the Director of NW Fashion District.

Project, DECA 2024–25

Rogue: JYP in India

Role
Lead Strategist
Client
JYP Entertainment

The vision

India has 1.4 billion people, 65% of them under 35, and a fast-growing K-pop fanbase, but little representation in global pop.

The idea

Rogue, an Indian girl group blending Indian culture with K-pop, backed by a market-expansion and brand strategy built on cultural analysis and trend forecasting.

Execution

Researched target markets, market entry and risk, then designed the business plan and a 30-slide pitch deck in one concert-lit violet system.

Result

4th statewide and an ICDC 2025 qualifier. The forecast was later validated when HYBE launched HYBE India.
